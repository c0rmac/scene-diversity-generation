Mug and spoon scenes

All R363 runs ·
Energy C is the value the solver minimises for a scene: the scene is released from rest for 1 s and C = Σmug, spoon max(largest displacement of a point of the body − 1 mm, 0) / 100 mm + contact penalties. C = 0 means the scene is at rest in that test. The residual is the largest displacement itself, in mm, in the 1 s release or in the 10 s final check (worst of the 2 ms and 1 ms time steps).
