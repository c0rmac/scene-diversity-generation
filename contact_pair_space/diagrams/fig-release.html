Each recording starts from a sampled scene at rest (zero velocity) and lets gravity act for three seconds. The two numbers are the largest distance any monitored point of each body has travelled from its starting place so far. A sampled scene has one contact between the bodies and one point of the pair on the table, which is not enough to hold it, so most scenes move a long way before they settle, and many end with the spoon lying apart from the mug. With the mug resting, in any of its stable postures or upright only, the mug starts at rest and the spoon touches it; the mug then usually stays where it is and only the spoon moves.

Samples 1 to 24 of the first figure for the chosen region, with no selection. Simulated in MuJoCo with friction coefficient 10, no added damping, a time step of 2 ms and every fourth step stored. “Still” means that no monitored point moved more than 1 mm during the last half second. The label of the end scene is a descriptive contact family, not a tested component.
