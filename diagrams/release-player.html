The blue and orange lines join a material point's position in the initial scene to where that same point reached its largest displacement so far, giving Xmug(s; τ) and Xspoon(s; τ). The maximum is taken over all monitored material points on the body's primitives and all times up to τ. The maximizing point can change, so an open circle can jump to another location on the initial body; its reference always remains the release pose. A line may end at an earlier pose if that maximum occurred earlier. Red marks locate the largest reported penetration so far; the enlarged bars show penetration depths. At τ = 1 s the running maxima give Xb(s) and δ(s).

δ0(s) measures overlap in the starting geometry and stays fixed. The ordinary starting scenes aim to have zero initial overlap. Show initial overlap loads a deliberately intersecting mug–spoon input, paused at τ = 0, to make a positive δ0 visible; it is a diagnostic example outside the touching-scene map.
