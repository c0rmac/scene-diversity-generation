Each dot is a material sample drawn at Rbp + tb in the current scene; the set moves with the body. The readout is the distance between the embeddings Ψ of the reference scene and the current one. The mug part combines the change of its attitude, weighted by the root-mean-square radius of its samples, with the change of its height; the spoon part is the exact mean squared displacement of its samples as seen from the mug, so a tilt that carries the spoon with the mug does not count for the spoon. The motion is constructed for illustration; the bodies are placed on the table and do not touch.
