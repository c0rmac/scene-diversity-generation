The blue and orange lines join a material point's position in the initial scene to where that same point reached its largest displacement so far, giving Xmug(s; τ) and Xspoon(s; τ). The maximum is taken over all monitored material points and all times up to τ, so the point can change and an open circle can jump to another place on the initial body. Red marks locate the largest penetration so far; the enlarged bars show penetration depths. At τ = 1 s the running maxima give Xb(s) and δ(s).

δ0(s) is the overlap in the starting geometry and stays fixed. Starting scenes built by the lowering have at most a small numerical overlap, within the 0.16 mm tolerance. Show initial overlap loads a deliberately intersecting input, paused at τ = 0, to make a positive δ0 visible; it is outside the touching-scene map.
