The arrows run from surface points of the two bodies to where one step in the chosen direction takes them, and the bodies swing back and forth along the same step. The step is shown with the gauge part removed: a step and the same step followed by a shift or turn of the whole scene count as one, and the figure shows the one that moves the material least. That is why a change of the offset moves the mug as well as the spoon, each by half. The list on the right gives, for this scene, the material length √(vᵀGv) of a unit step v in every direction, in millimetres; click an entry to show it. The heights from the lowering are not part of the metric and are not applied here.
