Both noises use the same 300 standard normal vectors z and the same amplitude a, so the two clouds are paired draw by draw. The cloud on the left shows each draw η along three eigen-directions of G̃ at this scene: the softest (the spoon rolling about its shaft), a middle one and the stiffest (the table above describes them). Measured in the coordinates, the isotropic draws fill a sphere and the shaped draws stretch along the soft direction; measured as material displacement, the shaped draws fill a sphere and the isotropic draws are squashed along the soft direction. The scene on the right is moved by the rigid motion of one draw, before the drift and the lowering, with the outline of the scene before the step.
